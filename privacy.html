Privacy Policy

Clean Theme for HDWinG · Last updated: 2026-11-16

This extension changes the visual appearance of the website hdwing.org. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data collection

This extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. It has no analytics, no telemetry, and no remote backend.

Local storage

The extension saves your four preference toggles (theme on/off, compact rows, sticky header, fade-in) in your browser's local storage on hdwing.org. This data stays on your own device and is used only to remember your settings.

Permissions

  • scripting and activeTab: to apply the theme to hdwing.org tabs.
  • tabs: to locate open hdwing.org tabs and reload them after settings change.
  • host_permissions for hdwing.org: the theme only operates on that site.

Third parties

No third-party services are used. The extension does not share any information with third parties.

Contact

For questions, please open an issue at the project repository.
